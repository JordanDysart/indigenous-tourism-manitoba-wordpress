# Skill: Marketing & Conversion Auditor

**Trigger:** When assigned to run a **marketing audit** on an individual page of `https://indigenoustourismmanitoba.ca/`.

**Objective:**
Evaluate how effectively the page converts visitors, guides them toward organizational objectives (discovering operators, booking cultural experiences, applying for membership, registering for training), clarifies page functionality, and removes user friction.

---

## Audit Checklist & Evaluation Criteria

### 1. Page Purpose & Value Proposition Clarity
- [ ] **5-Second Rule:** Is the primary message and purpose of the page clear within the first 5 seconds of viewing?
- [ ] **Compelling Headline & Subheading:** Does the hero section communicate a clear value proposition explaining what the user will discover or gain?
- [ ] **Clarity of Functionality:** Identify if the page lacks clear purpose or feels like an orphan/dead-end without actionable context.

### 2. Calls-to-Action (CTAs) & Conversion Architecture
- [ ] **Primary CTA:** Is there a clear, high-contrast, primary action (e.g. "Explore Operators", "Book Experience", "Join ITM", "Submit Inquiry") placed prominently above the fold?
- [ ] **Secondary CTAs:** Are there supporting conversion paths for users not ready for the primary action (e.g. "Download Travel Guide", "Sign up for Newsletter", "Follow on Instagram")?
- [ ] **Button Visual Hierarchy:** Do CTA buttons visually distinguish themselves from standard links, body text, or background elements?
- [ ] **Action-Oriented Copy:** Are button labels action-oriented and specific (e.g. "View Authentic Tours" vs "Click Here")?

### 3. User Journey, Engagement & Content Routing
- [ ] **Frictionless Pathway:** How many clicks or form fields stand between the user and completing their goal? Are there unnecessary barriers?
- [ ] **Related Links & Internal Cross-Selling:** Does the page guide the user to the next logical step (e.g., related regional operators, upcoming cultural events, complementary experiences)?
- [ ] **Dead-End Mitigation:** Does the page end with next steps or recommendations rather than abruptly dropping the user at the footer?

### 4. Trust, Social Proof & Storytelling
- [ ] **Authenticity & Social Proof:** Are there customer testimonials, operator spotlights, or community endorsements to build trust?
- [ ] **Credibility Markers:** Are partner badges, indigenous ownership accreditations, or recognized awards visible?
- [ ] **Visual Engagement:** Does the page use evocative imagery representing genuine Manitoba Indigenous culture and landscapes?

### 5. Search Engine & Social Share Readiness (Organic Reach)
- [ ] **Meta Title & Description:** Does the page have an enticing `<title>` and `<meta name="description">` that drives high click-through rates from search results?
- [ ] **Social Share Previews (Open Graph / Twitter Cards):** Does the page provide rich social preview titles, descriptions, and share images?

---

## Output Report Structure

When completing the audit for a page, output the findings in this format:

```markdown
# Marketing & Conversion Audit: [Page Title]
**URL:** [Page URL]
**Date:** [YYYY-MM-DD]
**Conversion Readiness:** [High / Moderate / Poor / Dead-End]

## Summary of Findings
[Brief 2-3 sentence overview of value proposition clarity, conversion bottlenecks, and visitor journey]

## Conversion Opportunities & Gaps

| # | Focus Area | Current State | Identified Gap / Opportunity | Recommended Solution |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Primary CTA | No button in hero | Visitor must scroll to bottom to find membership form | Add "Apply for Membership" button in hero banner |
| 2 | Social Proof | Plain text listing | No operator quotes or visitor testimonials | Add a 2-quote testimonial carousel |
| 3 | Page Exit / Dead End | Page ends at paragraph | No next steps or related articles | Add "Related Experiences" cross-links before footer |

## Strategic Recommendations
1. **[High Impact / Quick Win]:** ...
2. **[User Journey Enhancement]:** ...
3. **[Long-Term Growth Idea]:** ...
```
