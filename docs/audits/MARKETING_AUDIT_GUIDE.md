# Marketing & Conversion Audit Guide

This guide details the methodology for auditing conversion pathways, page purpose, messaging clarity, and user experience on the Indigenous Tourism Manitoba website.

---

## 1. Marketing Evaluation Criteria

### 1.1 Page Purpose & Value Proposition Clarity
- **The 5-Second Test:** Can a new visitor understand what this page offers within 5 seconds?
- **Headline Impact:** Does the H1 clearly state what the visitor will gain, learn, or experience?
- **Clarity of Functionality:** Identify pages that seem ambiguous in purpose (e.g. pages that provide information without guiding the user on what action to take next).
- **Target Audience Alignment:** Is the copy tailored appropriately (e.g. leisure travelers seeking authentic experiences vs. Indigenous operators seeking membership benefits)?

### 1.2 Calls-to-Action (CTAs) & Conversion Funnels
- **Primary CTA Above the Fold:**
  - Is there a prominent, high-contrast button in or immediately following the hero section?
  - Does the button text clearly state the benefit (e.g., "Explore Operators", "Apply for Membership", "Book a Cultural Tour", "Contact ITM")?
- **Secondary / Soft CTAs:**
  - For visitors not ready for the main conversion, are there alternative low-friction steps (e.g., "Download Guide", "Sign Up for Newsletter", "Explore Regional Map")?
- **CTA Visual Hierarchy:**
  - Are CTA buttons styled distinctively with brand accent colors so they command attention?
  - Are secondary actions styled as ghost or outline buttons to maintain visual priority?

### 1.3 Visitor Flow & User Journey Friction
- **Form Length & Friction:** Are lead capture and contact forms minimal and straightforward, or do they ask for unnecessary information upfront?
- **Dead-End Avoidance:** Does the bottom of the page offer next steps or related links (e.g. "Related Operators", "Explore Other Regions", "Upcoming Events") before the footer?
- **Cross-Linking:** Are relevant internal pages linked contextual within the body copy to keep users browsing?

### 1.4 Trust, Authenticity & Social Proof
- **Authenticity Indicators:** Visible community backing, Indigenous business ownership verification, or cultural authenticity badging.
- **Social Proof:** Real operator quotes, customer testimonials, high-impact photography of actual community guides, and partner endorsements.
- **Transparency:** Clear contact details, social media links, and physical territory acknowledgments.

### 1.5 Organic Search & Social Sharing Readiness
- **Search Engine Snippet:** Is the `<title>` (under 60 characters) and `<meta name="description">` (under 155 characters) compelling and click-worthy?
- **Social Sharing (OG Tags):** Are Open Graph tags (`og:title`, `og:description`, `og:image`) configured so sharing on Facebook, LinkedIn, or Twitter produces an engaging preview card?

---

## 2. Step-by-Step Testing Procedure

1. **First Impression & Value Prop Review:**
   - Review hero headline, imagery, and intro text for clarity and emotional resonance.
2. **CTA & Conversion Audit:**
   - Map all actionable buttons and links. Check placement, visual contrast, and action-oriented phrasing.
3. **Friction Analysis:**
   - Walk through the user's path to conversion (e.g. clicking an inquiry button, filling out a form).
4. **Social & SEO Meta Check:**
   - Extract `<title>`, `<meta name="description">`, and `og:*` tags.
5. **Formulate Recommendations:**
   - Prioritize recommendations into Quick Wins (immediate CTA copy/placement tweaks) and Strategic Growth opportunities.
