## 1. Executive Summary
The Step 2: 7-Day Training Course page delivers high marketing appeal by detailing recognized safety credentials (Wilderness First Aid, Food Safe) and hands-on field immersion. The primary marketing opportunities involve adding an on-page "Inquire & Register for Step 2" call-to-action button, displaying partner accreditation badges, and declaring `<meta name="description">` and social Open Graph tags.
