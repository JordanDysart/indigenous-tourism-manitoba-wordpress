# Audit Report: Shelly’s Bistro — Marketing & Conversion

**Audit Concern:** `marketing`  
**Date Audited:** `2026-08-30`  
**Conversion Readiness:** `Good (Authentic Community Charm; Direct Menu & Road Trip CTAs Recommended)`  

---

## 1. Executive Summary
The Shelly’s Bistro profile highlights an authentic Indigenous-owned culinary stop situated in the historic Métis settlement of St. Laurent along Lake Manitoba. Renowned for its fresh pickerel dinners and traditional bannock, conversion for highway road-trippers and local diners can be amplified by deploying menu view CTAs, adding culinary highlight badges, and publishing rich search/social graph metadata.

---

## 2. Detailed Findings

| # | Focus Area | Current State | Identified Gap / Opportunity | Recommended Solution |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Culinary Ordering Funnel | Single generic "Visit Website" button | Interlake road-trippers and diners want instant menu access and direct calling options. | Implement prominent dual CTAs: *"View Menu & Daily Specials"* & *"Call to Order / Inquire"*. |
| 2 | Experience Highlights Ribbon | Embedded in paragraph copy | Visual badges (Fresh Lake Pickerel, Traditional Bannock, Métis Comfort Classics, Road Trip Stop) communicate bistro identity immediately. | Add a "Bistro Highlights" badge ribbon to the sidebar overview. |
| 3 | Road Trip Mapping & Location | Address in text | St. Laurent is a prime road trip stop along MB-6; a quick "Get Driving Directions" Google Maps link increases foot traffic. | Add a direct "Get Directions" link in the overview. |
| 4 | SEO & Search Snippet | `<meta name="description">` missing | Search engines lack a rich description for this regional bistro. | Add a targeted meta description (e.g. "Savour fresh Lake Manitoba pickerel, traditional bannock, and Métis comfort hospitality at Shelly's Bistro in St. Laurent, Manitoba."). |
| 5 | Social Share Previews | Open Graph / Twitter cards missing | Shared links render generic social preview cards without imagery. | Implement standard `og:title`, `og:description`, and `og:image`. |
