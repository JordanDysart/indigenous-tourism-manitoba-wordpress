# Audit Report: Contact Us — Marketing & Conversion

**Page URL:** `https://indigenoustourismmanitoba.ca/contact-us/`  
**Audit Concern:** `marketing`  
**Date Audited:** `2026-08-30`  
**Conversion Readiness:** `Good (Multi-Channel Inbound Contact; Response Time Reassurance Needed)`  

---

## 1. Executive Summary
The Contact Us page serves as the primary organizational inquiry channel for tourists, Indigenous operators, industry partners, and media. The page offers excellent contact transparency with direct phone numbers, email, physical address, and an embedded form. Key marketing enhancements include setting explicit response time expectations, offering departmental routing options on the form, and declaring `<meta name="description">` and social Open Graph tags.

---

## 2. Detailed Findings

| # | Focus Area | Current State | Identified Gap / Opportunity | Recommended Solution |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Inbound Lead Routing | Single general contact form | Inquiries from tourists, prospective member operators, and media are mixed into one inbox. | Add an "Inquiry Type" dropdown (Tourism Questions, Membership & Business Support, Guide Training, Media/Press). |
| 2 | Response Time Expectation | No turnaround mentioned | Visitors are unsure how quickly their inquiry will be addressed. | Add reassuring microcopy: *"We typically reply to all inquiries within 1–2 business days."* |
| 3 | Newsletter Sign-up Cross-Sell | Standalone contact form | Missed opportunity to capture contact inquiries into the monthly marketing newsletter list. | Add an opt-in checkbox: *"Keep me updated on Indigenous experiences and events in Manitoba."* |
| 4 | SEO & Search Snippet | `<meta name="description">` missing | Search engines lack a concise summary of ITM's contact and office coordinates. | Add a targeted meta description (e.g. "Get in touch with Indigenous Tourism Manitoba. Contact our Winnipeg office for operator support, travel guidance, and partnership inquiries."). |
| 5 | Social Share Previews | Open Graph / Twitter cards missing | Shared links render generic social preview cards without imagery. | Implement standard `og:title`, `og:description`, and `og:image`. |

---

## 3. Checklist Verification

- [x] **Multi-Channel Contact Options:** Phone, email, address, and interactive form available.
- [x] **Social Media Links:** Facebook, Instagram, and LinkedIn channels linked.
- [ ] **Response SLA Microcopy:** Turnaround time expectation is missing.
- [ ] **Search Engine Metadata:** Meta description is missing.
- [ ] **Social Media Meta Tags:** Open Graph tags are missing.

---

## 4. Prioritized Action Plan

### 🔴 High Priority / Immediate Fixes
1. **Implement Meta Description & OG Tags:** Add targeted 155-character description and Open Graph tags to `<head>`.
2. **Add Response Time Reassurance:** Insert *"Our team responds to all inquiries within 1–2 business days"* above the form submit button.

### 🟡 Medium Priority / Improvements
1. **Add Departmental Routing Field:** Introduce a Subject / Department dropdown selector in Gravity Forms.
2. **Newsletter Opt-in Checkbox:** Enable users to subscribe to ITM news directly from the contact form.

### 🟢 Low Priority / Polish & Recommendations
1. **Custom Confirmation Screen:** Direct form submissions to a personalized thank-you confirmation highlighting featured experiences.
