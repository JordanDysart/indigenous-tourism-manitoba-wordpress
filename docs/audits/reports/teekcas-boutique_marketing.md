# Audit Report: Teekca’s Boutique — Marketing & Conversion

**Page URL:** `https://indigenoustourismmanitoba.ca/operator/teekcas-boutique/`  
**Audit Concern:** `marketing`  
**Date Audited:** `2026-08-30`  
**Conversion Readiness:** `Action Required (Critical Conversion Blocker: Dead Website Link; Dual In-Store & E-Commerce CTAs Recommended)`  

---

## 1. Executive Summary
Teekca’s Boutique is one of Manitoba’s most recognized Indigenous retail destinations, operating physical boutiques at The Forks Market and St. Vital Centre in Winnipeg alongside a nationwide online e-commerce platform. **Critical Conversion Failure:** The single primary outbound CTA points to `https://teekcasboutique.com/`, which is offline (connection refused). Updating this link to their live e-commerce site **Teekca's Atâwêkamik** (`https://www.teekcasatawekamik.com`) and deploying distinct "Shop Online" vs. "Visit In-Store" CTAs will immediately restore and multiply conversion.

---

## 2. Detailed Findings

| # | Focus Area | Current State | Identified Gap / Opportunity | Recommended Solution |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Primary Conversion Link | Points to `teekcasboutique.com` (Offline) | **Critical Blocker:** 100% of outbound clicks fail due to connection refused. | Immediately update website destination to `https://www.teekcasatawekamik.com`. |
| 2 | Dual Conversion Funnel | Single generic button | Shoppers need separate pathways for online e-commerce orders vs. visiting physical stores at The Forks. | Implement dual CTAs: *"Shop Online at Teekca's Atâwêkamik"* & *"Visit at The Forks Market"*. |

| 5 | Social Share Previews | Open Graph / Twitter cards missing | Shared links render generic social preview cards without imagery. | Implement standard `og:title`, `og:description`, and `og:image`. |
