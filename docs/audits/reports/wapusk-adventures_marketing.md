## 1. Executive Summary
The Wapusk Adventures operator profile features an internationally recognized Indigenous (Métis) dog mushing and eco-adventure company founded by Dave Daley in Churchill, Manitoba. The business offers bucket-list dog sledding, summer tundra cart runs, and northern lights viewing. Conversion rates can be greatly boosted by introducing direct "Book Dog Sled Adventure" CTAs, highlighting adventure badges, and deploying full SEO and Open Graph metadata.

---

## 2. Detailed Findings

| # | Focus Area | Current State | Identified Gap / Opportunity | Recommended Solution |
