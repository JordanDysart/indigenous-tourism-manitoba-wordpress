## 1. Executive Summary
The Whiteshell Petroforms Authentic Indigenous Tours profile presents an award-winning cultural guiding enterprise at Manidoo-Abi in Whiteshell Provincial Park. With profound Anishinaabe teachings and authentic Elder-led tours, traveler conversion can be substantially elevated by introducing direct "Book Guided Tour" action buttons, highlighting the Indigenous Tourism Award badge, and deploying rich search/social graph metadata.

## 2. Detailed Findings

| # | Focus Area | Current State | Identified Gap / Opportunity | Recommended Solution |

| 5 | Social Share Previews | Open Graph / Twitter cards missing | Shared links render generic social preview cards without imagery. | Implement standard `og:title`, `og:description`, and `og:image`. |
