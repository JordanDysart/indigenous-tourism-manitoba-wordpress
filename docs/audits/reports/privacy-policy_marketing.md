# Audit Report: Privacy Policy — Marketing & Trust

**Page URL:** `https://indigenoustourismmanitoba.ca/privacy-policy/`  
**Audit Concern:** `marketing`  
**Date Audited:** `2026-08-30`  
**Compliance & Trust Readiness:** `Good (Clear Governance Provisions; Metadata Gaps)`  

---

## 1. Executive Summary
The Privacy Policy page functions primarily as an organizational trust and regulatory compliance asset, reassuring members, visitors, and partners that their personal data is protected under Canadian privacy standards. Key marketing improvements include adding a meta description and Open Graph tags, ensuring seamless cross-linking to the Contact Us page, and maintaining clear CASL / newsletter opt-out guidelines.

---

## 2. Detailed Findings

| # | Focus Area | Current State | Identified Gap / Opportunity | Recommended Solution |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Trust & Regulatory Signals | PIPEDA / CASL disclosures | Clear explanation of user data rights, newsletter subscription handling, and third-party tools. | Maintain transparent, plain-language privacy clauses. |
| 2 | Contact & Inquiry Linkage | Text email mention | Users wishing to exercise privacy rights or opt out can benefit from a direct link to `/contact-us/`. | Embed a direct clickable link to the Contact page in Section 4. |
| 3 | SEO & Search Snippet | `<meta name="description">` missing | Search engines lack a concise summary of the privacy policy. | Add a targeted meta description (e.g. "Read Indigenous Tourism Manitoba's privacy policy regarding data collection, cookies, and personal information protection."). |
| 4 | Social Share Previews | Open Graph / Twitter cards missing | Shared links render generic social preview cards without imagery. | Implement standard `og:title`, `og:description`, and `og:image`. |
