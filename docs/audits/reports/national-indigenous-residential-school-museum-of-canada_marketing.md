---

## 1. Executive Summary
The National Indigenous Residential School Museum of Canada (NIRSM) profile presents a site of national historical significance and survivor resilience at the former Portage la Prairie Indian Residential School on Keeshkeemaquah reserve. Conversion can be significantly elevated by adding clear educational tour booking pathways, showcasing National Historic Site accreditation badges, and deploying targeted search and social graph metadata.

---

## 2. Detailed Findings

| # | Focus Area | Current State | Identified Gap / Opportunity | Recommended Solution |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Educational Tour Booking Funnel | Single generic "Visit Website" button | School districts, universities, corporate teams, and travelers seek direct inquiry channels to book survivor-guided tours. | Implement prominent dual CTAs: *"Book a Guided Educational Tour"* & *"Plan Your Museum Visit"*. |
| 2 | National Historic Site Badges | Mentioned in paragraph copy | Visual badges (National Historic Site, Survivor-Led Tours, Commemorative Memorial, Archival Exhibits) immediately convey national importance. | Add a "Museum & Site Highlights" badge ribbon to the sidebar overview. |
| 3 | Group & Delegation Inquiries | General contact info | Highlighting educational packages and protocol for large delegation visits supports institutional trip planning. | Add an "Educational & Group Delegations" informational callout box. |
| 4 | SEO & Search Snippet | `<meta name="description">` missing | Search engines lack a rich description for this pivotal national commemorative site. | Add a targeted meta description (e.g. "Visit the National Indigenous Residential School Museum of Canada at Keeshkeemaquah, MB. Experience survivor-guided tours, truth and reconciliation educational exhibits, and national commemorative monuments."). |
| 5 | Social Share Previews | Open Graph / Twitter cards missing | Shared links render generic social preview cards without imagery. | Implement standard `og:title`, `og:description`, and `og:image`. |
