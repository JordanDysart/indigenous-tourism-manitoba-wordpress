# Audit Report: Tour and Related Services Category Archive — Marketing & Conversion

**Page URL:** `https://indigenoustourismmanitoba.ca/operator-category/tour-and-related-services/`  
**Audit Concern:** `marketing`  
**Date Audited:** `2026-08-30`  
**Conversion Readiness:** `Moderate (Compelling Cultural Guided Experiences; Missing Category Filter Switcher, Experience Format Badges, & SEO Meta)`  

---

## 1. Executive Summary
The Tour and Related Services operator category archive features guided cultural tours and Treaty education centres across Manitoba (Whiteshell Petroforms Authentic Indigenous Tours and Agowiidiwinan Centre). While listing cards link directly to individual operator profiles, the directory lacks horizontal category switcher pills, tour format micro-badges (e.g., outdoor walking tours vs. museum exhibits), departure location context, and essential search engine meta descriptions and Open Graph social sharing tags.

---

## 2. Detailed Findings

| # | Focus Area | Current State | Identified Gap / Opportunity | Recommended Solution |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Category Navigation & Discovery | Static 2-card grid | Cultural travelers cannot easily switch between categories (e.g., jump from Tours to Attractions or Accommodation). | Implement horizontal category filter switcher pills above the card grid. |
| 2 | Card Micro-Copy & Format Badges | Image and business title only | Cards do not immediately communicate excursion format (guided outdoor petroform hike vs. interpretive centre visit). | Render micro-badges beneath titles (e.g. *"Sacred Petroform Walking Tours • Whiteshell"*, *"Treaty Relations Heritage Centre • The Forks"*). |
| 3 | Tour Map & Itinerary Linkage | Absent on category page | Tourists planning regional road trips and itineraries lack geographical context on tour departure locations. | Add a banner CTA linking to the interactive Experience Map and noting booking lead times. |
| 4 | SEO & Search Snippet | `<meta name="description">` missing | Search engines lack a rich description for Manitoba Indigenous guided tours, Treaty education, and cultural walks. | Add a targeted meta description (e.g. "Discover guided Indigenous tours and Treaty relations programs in Manitoba. Book sacred petroform walks and cultural education experiences."). |
| 5 | Social Share Previews | Open Graph / Twitter cards missing | Shared links render generic social cards without category imagery. | Implement standard `og:title`, `og:description`, and `og:image` tags. |
