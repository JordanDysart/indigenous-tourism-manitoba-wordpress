## 1. Executive Summary
The Step 1: Introduction page provides a clear breakdown of foundational guide competencies, prerequisites, and sequential pagination ("← Back to Program Hub", "Next: Step 2 — 7-Day Training →"). The main marketing enhancements involve adding a direct "Apply / Inquire for Step 1" button at the bottom of the prerequisites section and declaring `<meta name="description">` and social Open Graph tags.
