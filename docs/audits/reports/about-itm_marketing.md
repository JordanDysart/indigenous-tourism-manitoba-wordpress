# Audit Report: About Indigenous Tourism Manitoba — Marketing & Conversion

**Page URL:** `https://indigenoustourismmanitoba.ca/about-itm/`  
**Audit Concern:** `marketing`  
**Date Audited:** `2026-08-29`  
**Conversion Readiness:** `Moderate (Dead-End at Page Bottom)`  

---

## 1. Executive Summary
The About ITM page successfully articulates the organization's mission, values, and brand origin ("Adventure to Understanding"). However, from a conversion and user journey perspective, the page ends abruptly: the final section ("Join the Indigenous Tourism Movement") contains no primary Call-to-Action button to guide readers to become a member, contact leadership, or explore operators. Additionally, `<meta name="description">` and social Open Graph tags are missing, and the `<title>` tag contains redundant brand repetition.

---

## 2. Detailed Findings

| # | Focus Area | Current State | Identified Gap / Opportunity | Recommended Solution |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Page Exit / Dead End | Bottom section "Join the Indigenous Tourism Movement" | Page ends with body text and drops directly into the footer without an actionable next step. | Add a dual conversion CTA banner at the bottom: "Become an ITM Member" (Primary) and "Meet Our Team" (Secondary). |
| 2 | SEO & Search Snippet | `<meta name="description">` missing | Search engines pull random sentence fragments for search results. | Add a tailored meta description (e.g. "Learn about Indigenous Tourism Manitoba (ITM), our mission to empower Indigenous entrepreneurs, and how we foster authentic cultural experiences across Manitoba."). |
| 3 | Title Tag Optimization | `<title>About Indigenous Tourism Manitoba – Indigenous Tourism Manitoba</title>` | Title tag repeats "Indigenous Tourism Manitoba" twice, wasting character budget. | Simplify title to: `About Us – Indigenous Tourism Manitoba` or `Our Mission & Story – Indigenous Tourism Manitoba`. |
| 4 | Social Share Preview | Open Graph / Twitter cards missing | Shared links on social platforms fail to generate image previews or rich snippets. | Implement standard `og:title`, `og:description`, and `og:image`. |
| 5 | Cross-Linking | Body copy lacks contextual internal links | Mentions of operators and leadership lack direct links to `/our-team/` and `/operators/`. | Add contextual hyperlinks inside the copy to retain visitor engagement. |

---

## 3. Checklist Verification

- [x] **Value Proposition Clarity:** Strong opening narrative explaining ITM's mandate and vision.
- [ ] **Search Engine Metadata:** Meta description is missing; title tag is repetitive.
- [ ] **Social Media Meta Tags:** Open Graph tags are missing.
- [ ] **Actionable CTAs:** No primary conversion buttons exist within the body content.
- [x] **Brand Authenticity:** Evocative storytelling aligned with the provincial tourism mandate.

---

## 4. Prioritized Action Plan

### 🔴 High Priority / Immediate Fixes
1. **Add Bottom Conversion Banner:** Insert a CTA block at the conclusion of "Join the Indigenous Tourism Movement" with buttons linking to `/become-a-member/` and `/contact-us/`.
2. **Add Meta Description:** Implement a 155-character search description for the page.

### 🟡 Medium Priority / Improvements
1. **Clean Up Title Tag:** Adjust page SEO title to avoid redundant brand duplication.
2. **Link Internal Narrative:** Hyperlink mentions of the Board of Directors to `/our-team/` and mentions of cultural tours to `/operators/`.

### 🟢 Low Priority / Polish & Recommendations
1. **Partner Accreditations:** Display partner accreditation badges (Indigenous Tourism Association of Canada - ITAC, Travel Manitoba) to enhance industry authority.
