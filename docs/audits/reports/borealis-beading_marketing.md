**Date Audited:** `2026-08-30`

---

## 1. Executive Summary
The Borealis Beading profile presents an intimate, culturally enriching workshop experience led by Métis artisan Melanie Gamache in Sainte-Geneviève, MB. While the operator narrative and photography convey authentic craft immersion, conversion can be elevated by introducing an explicit "Book a Workshop" CTA, displaying distance from Winnipeg for day-trippers, and deploying standard search engine and social graph metadata.

---

## 2. Detailed Findings

| # | Focus Area | Current State | Identified Gap / Opportunity | Recommended Solution |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Workshop Conversion Funnel | Single generic "Visit Website" button | Experiential tourists seek direct booking channels for hands-on beading and quillwork circles. | Implement high-intent dual CTAs: *"Book a Workshop"* & *"Visit Official Website"*. |
| 2 | Day-Trip Planning Clarity | Address lists "Sainte-Geneviève, MB" | Tourists unfamiliar with rural Manitoba need travel context (distance/drive time from Winnipeg). | Add a helpful note: *"Located just 35 minutes east of Winnipeg in Sainte-Geneviève"*. |
| 3 | Cultural Trust Badging | Narrative mentions authentic Métis heritage | Visual badges ("Certified Indigenous Artisan", "Hands-On Cultural Workshop") increase trust and booking intent. | Add trust badges to the sidebar overview. |
| 4 | SEO & Search Snippet | `<meta name="description">` missing | Search engines lack a rich description of hands-on Métis beading workshops in Manitoba. | Add a targeted meta description (e.g. "Experience authentic hands-on Métis beading, porcupine quillwork, and storytelling workshops with Melanie Gamache at Borealis Beading in Sainte-Geneviève, Manitoba."). |
| 5 | Social Share Previews | Open Graph / Twitter cards missing | Shared links render generic social preview cards without imagery. | Implement standard `og:title`, `og:description`, and `og:image`. |
