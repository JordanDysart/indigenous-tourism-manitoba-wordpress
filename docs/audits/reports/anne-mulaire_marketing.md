# Audit Report: Anne Mulaire — Marketing & Conversion

**Page URL:** `https://indigenoustourismmanitoba.ca/operator/anne-mulaire/`  
**Audit Concern:** `marketing`  
**Date Audited:** `2026-08-30`  
**Conversion Readiness:** `Good (Compelling Brand Narrative; Dual Retail/Online CTA Opportunities)`  

---

## 1. Executive Summary
The Anne Mulaire profile presents an inspiring blend of Métis cultural heritage and sustainable zero-waste fashion. Conversion performance is fundamentally solid with an active e-commerce website link (`https://annemulaire.ca/`). Key marketing opportunities include providing dual call-to-actions distinguishing online shopping from in-person boutique visits, adding store operating hours, and implementing search and social graph metadata.

---

## 2. Detailed Findings

| # | Focus Area | Current State | Identified Gap / Opportunity | Recommended Solution |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Dual Conversion Funnel | Single "Visit Website" button | Does not differentiate online shoppers from travelers looking for an in-person boutique visit in Winnipeg. | Implement dual CTAs: *"Shop Online"* & *"Visit Boutique (Map & Hours)"*. |
| 2 | Ethical / Cultural Badges | Text mentions zero-waste & Métis heritage | Visual badges ("Certified Indigenous Business", "Zero-Waste Sustainable Fashion") increase trust and conversion. | Add trust badges to the sidebar overview. |
| 3 | Category Discovery | "Workshops, Art & Culture" taxonomy tag | Connects tourists to other Indigenous makers and artisanal workshops. | High category synergy. |
| 4 | SEO & Search Snippet | `<meta name="description">` missing | Search engines lack a rich description of this award-winning Métis fashion house. | Add a targeted meta description (e.g. "Discover Anne Mulaire, an award-winning Métis eco-luxury fashion brand in Winnipeg offering sustainable, Canadian-made apparel and bespoke outerwear."). |
| 5 | Social Share Previews | Open Graph / Twitter cards missing | Shared links render generic social preview cards without imagery. | Implement standard `og:title`, `og:description`, and `og:image`. |
