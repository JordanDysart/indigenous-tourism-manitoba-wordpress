# Audit Report: Outdoors and Adventures Category Archive — Marketing & Conversion

**Page URL:** `https://indigenoustourismmanitoba.ca/operator-category/outdoors-and-adventures/`  
**Audit Concern:** `marketing`  
**Date Audited:** `2026-08-30`  
**Conversion Readiness:** `Moderate (Compelling Wilderness Experiences; Missing Category Filter Pills, Experience Map Link, & SEO Meta)`  

---

## 1. Executive Summary
The Outdoors and Adventures operator category archive showcases exceptional Indigenous wilderness experiences across Manitoba, from sub-Arctic dog sledding in Churchill (Wapusk Adventures) to boreal eco-tours and paddleboarding in the Interlake (Nature’s Edge Tourism). To increase visitor tour bookings, seasonal inquiries, and geographic discovery, the archive requires category filter navigation pills, regional activity badges on cards, a promotional CTA for the interactive Experience Map, and full search engine/social meta tags.

---

## 2. Detailed Findings

| # | Focus Area | Current State | Identified Gap / Opportunity | Recommended Solution |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Category Navigation & Discovery | Static 2-card grid | Adventure travelers cannot easily switch between categories or filter by northern vs. southern Manitoba experiences. | Add horizontal category switcher pills (Outdoors, Attractions, Accommodation, Culinary, Events) and regional filters. |
| 2 | Card Micro-Copy & Badging | Image and business title only | Cards lack immediate context regarding adventure type, seasonality, and destination region. | Render micro-badges beneath titles (e.g. *"Eco-Tours & SUP • Interlake"*, *"Dog Sledding & Aurora Tours • Churchill"*). |
| 3 | Experience Map Integration | Absent on category page | Wilderness travelers strongly rely on geographical map planning for remote expeditions. | Add a banner CTA: *"Explore Outdoor Adventures on the Interactive Experience Map"* linking to `/experience-map/?category=outdoors-and-adventures`. |
| 4 | SEO & Search Snippet | `<meta name="description">` missing | Search engines lack a rich summary for Manitoba Indigenous wilderness tours and eco-adventures. | Add a targeted meta description (e.g. "Discover authentic Indigenous outdoor adventures in Manitoba. Experience Churchill dog sledding, northern eco-tours, and guided paddleboarding."). |
| 5 | Social Share Previews | Open Graph / Twitter cards missing | Shared links render generic social preview cards without category imagery. | Implement standard `og:title`, `og:description`, and `og:image` tags. |
