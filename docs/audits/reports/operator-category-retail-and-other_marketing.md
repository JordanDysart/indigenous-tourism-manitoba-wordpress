# Audit Report: Retail and Other Category Archive — Marketing & Conversion

**Page URL:** `https://indigenoustourismmanitoba.ca/operator-category/retail-and-other/`  
**Audit Concern:** `marketing`  
**Date Audited:** `2026-08-30`  
**Conversion Readiness:** `Moderate (High-Quality Artisan Brands; Missing Category Filter Switcher, Format Badges, & SEO Meta)`  

---

## 1. Executive Summary
The Retail and Other operator category archive showcases authentic Indigenous and Métis fashion, crafts, and gift boutiques across Manitoba (Anne Mulaire and Teekca’s Boutique). While the directory links cleanly to operator profiles, it lacks category filter switcher pills, format badges (e.g. eco-fashion vs. artisan giftware), physical shopping location cues (The Forks Market, St. Boniface), and essential search engine meta descriptions and Open Graph social sharing tags.

---

## 2. Detailed Findings

| # | Focus Area | Current State | Identified Gap / Opportunity | Recommended Solution |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Category Navigation & Discovery | Static 2-card grid | Shoppers cannot easily switch between categories (e.g., jump from Retail to Culinary or Attractions). | Implement horizontal category filter switcher pills above the card grid. |
| 2 | Card Micro-Copy & Format Badges | Image and business title only | Cards do not immediately indicate retail product specialty or brick-and-mortar storefront locations. | Render micro-badges beneath titles (e.g. *"Zero-Waste Métis Fashion • St. Boniface"*, *"Artisan Crafts & Mukluks • The Forks Market"*). |
| 3 | Shopping Map & Online Store Linkage | Absent on category page | Tourists seeking in-person shopping or online ordering lack quick directional cues. | Add a banner CTA linking to the interactive Experience Map and highlighting e-commerce availability. |
| 4 | SEO & Search Snippet | `<meta name="description">` missing | Search engines lack a rich description for Manitoba Indigenous fashion, artisan crafts, and gifts. | Add a targeted meta description (e.g. "Discover authentic Indigenous and Métis fashion, handcrafted jewelry, mukluks, and artisan gifts in Manitoba."). |
| 5 | Social Share Previews | Open Graph / Twitter cards missing | Shared links render generic social cards without category imagery. | Implement standard `og:title`, `og:description`, and `og:image` tags. |

---

## 3. Checklist Verification

- [x] **Value Proposition:** Direct showcase of premier Indigenous fashion and retail boutiques in Manitoba.
- [x] **Card Clickability:** Both operator cards route directly to detailed operator profiles.
- [ ] **Category Cross-Navigation:** Lacks filter tabs or category switcher pills.
- [ ] **Experience Map Linkage:** No direct link to view retail storefronts on the interactive map.
- [ ] **Search Engine Metadata:** Meta description is missing.
- [ ] **Social Media Meta Tags:** Open Graph tags are missing.
