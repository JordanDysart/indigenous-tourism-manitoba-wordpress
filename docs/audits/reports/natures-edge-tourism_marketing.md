# Audit Report: Nature’s Edge Tourism — Marketing & Conversion

**Page URL:** `https://indigenoustourismmanitoba.ca/operator/natures-edge-tourism/`  
**Audit Concern:** `marketing`  
**Date Audited:** `2026-08-30`  
**Conversion Readiness:** `Good (Exciting Eco-Adventure Appeal; Direct Booking CTAs Recommended)`  

---

## 1. Executive Summary
The Nature’s Edge Tourism profile presents an appealing outdoor recreation operator offering certified stand-up paddleboarding (SUP) lessons, sunset paddle tours, SUP yoga, and equipment rentals in Lac du Bonnet. Conversion can be significantly boosted by implementing direct tour booking and equipment rental action buttons, displaying experience badges, and adding targeted search/social graph metadata.

---

## 2. Detailed Findings

| # | Focus Area | Current State | Identified Gap / Opportunity | Recommended Solution |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Adventure Booking Funnel | Single generic "Visit Website" button | Travelers looking to paddle seek immediate pathways to book guided tours or reserve rental gear. | Implement prominent dual CTAs: *"Book a Guided Paddle Tour"* & *"Reserve SUP Rentals"*. |
| 2 | Experience Highlight Badges | Mentioned in paragraph text | Visual badges (Certified SUP Instruction, Sunset Tours, SUP Yoga, Board Rentals, Group Bookings) quickly highlight offerings. | Add an "Adventure Highlights" badge ribbon to the sidebar overview. |
| 3 | Seasonal Operating Window | Implicit in tour text | Visitors need clear seasonal availability cues (e.g. June – September lake season) to plan summer excursions. | Add a "Seasonality & Weather" informational badge. |
| 4 | SEO & Search Snippet | `<meta name="description">` missing | Search engines lack a rich description for this outdoor adventure provider. | Add a targeted meta description (e.g. "Experience guided stand-up paddleboarding (SUP) lessons, sunset tours, and board rentals with Nature's Edge Tourism in Lac du Bonnet, Manitoba."). |
| 5 | Social Share Previews | Open Graph / Twitter cards missing | Shared links render generic social preview cards without imagery. | Implement standard `og:title`, `og:description`, and `og:image`. |
