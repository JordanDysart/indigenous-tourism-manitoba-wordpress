## 1. Executive Summary
The Spence Custom Carving profile presents the master craftsmanship of Indigenous artist Fred Spence, specializing in custom handcrafted wildlife carvings, cultural totem sculptures, and interactive carving workshops. Conversion can be significantly accelerated by introducing direct commission and workshop booking action buttons, adding artisan badges, and publishing rich search/social graph metadata.
