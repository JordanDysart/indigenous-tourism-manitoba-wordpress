## 1. Executive Summary
The Turtle Village profile highlights a luxury glamping and geodesic eco-cabin retreat near Grand Beach on Lake Winnipeg. With unique turtle-shell dome architecture, off-grid solar sustainability, and boreal lakeside relaxation, guest conversion can be substantially elevated by introducing direct "Book Cabin Stay" reservation CTAs, showcasing key amenity badges, and deploying rich search/social graph metadata.

---

## 2. Detailed Findings

| # | Focus Area | Current State | Identified Gap / Opportunity | Recommended Solution |

| 1 | Accommodation Booking Funnel | Single generic "Visit Website" link | Travelers seeking cabin getaways expect immediate booking and date-check functionality. | Implement prominent primary CTA: *"Book Your Cabin / Check Dates"* directing into the reservation flow. |

| 5 | Social Share Previews | Open Graph / Twitter cards missing | Shared links render generic social preview cards without imagery. | Implement standard `og:title`, `og:description`, and `og:image`. |
