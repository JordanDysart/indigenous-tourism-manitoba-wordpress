## 1. Executive Summary
The Sharecuterie profile presents a modern Indigenous-owned artisanal charcuterie and grazing enterprise based in Winnipeg. The narrative highlights custom grazing boards, corporate event platters, and luxury picnic boxes. Conversion can be significantly increased by deploying high-intent culinary ordering buttons, adding service badges, and publishing rich search/social graph metadata.
