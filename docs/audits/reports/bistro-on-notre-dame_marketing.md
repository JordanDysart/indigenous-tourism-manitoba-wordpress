## 1. Executive Summary
The Bistro on Notre Dame operator profile positions this Indigenous-owned Winnipeg restaurant as a must-visit culinary destination for travelers seeking authentic local ingredients, bison, pickerel, and freshly baked bannock. Conversion velocity can be significantly enhanced by adding distinct "View Menu" and "Reserve a Table" action prompts, highlighting dining hours, and implementing search engine and social graph metadata.

---

## 2. Detailed Findings

| # | Focus Area | Current State | Identified Gap / Opportunity | Recommended Solution |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Culinary Conversion Funnel | Single generic "Visit Website" button | Food tourists seek immediate menu previews and reservation capabilities. | Implement high-intent dual CTAs: *"View Dining Menu"* & *"Reserve a Table"*. |
| 2 | Dining Hours & Experience Clarity | Hours omitted from overview sidebar | Visitors need to know lunch vs. dinner service hours and patio availability. | Insert a concise "Dining Hours & Location" info card. |
| 3 | Culinary Trust Badging | Narrative mentions local Manitoba growers | Visual badges ("Indigenous-Owned Restaurant", "Manitoba Farm-to-Table", "Locally Sourced") amplify culinary appeal. | Add culinary trust badges to the sidebar overview. |
| 4 | SEO & Search Snippet | `<meta name="description">` missing | Search engines lack a rich description of Indigenous dining in Winnipeg. | Add a targeted meta description (e.g. "Experience authentic Indigenous farm-to-table dining at Bistro on Notre Dame in Winnipeg, featuring Manitoba bison, fresh pickerel, and traditional bannock."). |
| 5 | Social Share Previews | Open Graph / Twitter cards missing | Shared links render generic social preview cards without food imagery. | Implement standard `og:title`, `og:description`, and `og:image`. |
