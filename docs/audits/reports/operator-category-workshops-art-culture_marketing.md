# Audit Report: Workshops, Art & Culture Category Archive — Marketing & Conversion

**Page URL:** `https://indigenoustourismmanitoba.ca/operator-category/workshops-art-culture/`  
**Audit Concern:** `marketing`  
**Date Audited:** `2026-08-30`  
**Conversion Readiness:** `Moderate (High-Appeal Cultural Craft & Workshop Offerings; Missing Category Filter Switcher, Craft Medium Badges, & SEO Meta)`  

---

## 1. Executive Summary
The Workshops, Art & Culture operator category archive showcases traditional Indigenous artistry, hands-on craft workshops, and cultural preservation institutions across Manitoba (Spence Custom Carving, Borealis Beading, and Manitoba Indigenous Cultural Education Centre Inc.). While listing cards link directly to individual operator profiles, the directory lacks horizontal category switcher pills, craft medium micro-badges (e.g. soapstone carving vs. traditional Métis beadwork), location context, and essential search engine meta descriptions and Open Graph social sharing tags.

---

## 2. Detailed Findings

| # | Focus Area | Current State | Identified Gap / Opportunity | Recommended Solution |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Category Navigation & Discovery | Static 3-card grid | Cultural travelers and workshop participants cannot easily switch between categories (e.g., jump from Workshops to Attractions or Culinary). | Implement horizontal category filter switcher pills above the card grid. |
| 2 | Card Micro-Copy & Craft Badges | Image and business title only | Cards do not immediately communicate craft disciplines or workshop formats. | Render micro-badges beneath titles (e.g. *"Soapstone Carving & Studio Workshops"*, *"Traditional Métis Beadwork"*, *"Indigenous Library & Heritage Programs"*). |
| 3 | Workshop Map & Studio Finder | Absent on category page | Visitors looking for studio locations and regional workshops lack quick geographical routing. | Add a banner CTA linking to the interactive Experience Map and noting booking lead times for classes. |
| 4 | SEO & Search Snippet | `<meta name="description">` missing | Search engines lack a rich description for Manitoba Indigenous art workshops, carving lessons, and Métis beadwork. | Add a targeted meta description (e.g. "Discover authentic Indigenous art, soapstone carving, and Métis beadwork workshops in Manitoba. Explore cultural learning experiences and heritage studios."). |
| 5 | Social Share Previews | Open Graph / Twitter cards missing | Shared links render generic social cards without category imagery. | Implement standard `og:title`, `og:description`, and `og:image` tags. |
