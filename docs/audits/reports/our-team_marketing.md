# Audit Report: Our Team — Marketing & Conversion

**Page URL:** `https://indigenoustourismmanitoba.ca/our-team/`  
**Audit Concern:** `marketing`  
**Date Audited:** `2026-08-29`  
**Conversion Readiness:** `Good`  

---

## 1. Executive Summary
The Our Team page provides strong organizational credibility and social proof, clearly establishing Indigenous Tourism Manitoba's leadership across both operational staff and community-rooted Board Directors. The page features explicit conversion actions near the top ("Contact Us" and "Become a Member"). Main marketing improvements focus on fixing the missing search engine `<meta name="description">`, adding Open Graph social sharing tags, and surfacing community affiliations on team cards.

---

## 2. Detailed Findings

| # | Focus Area | Current State | Identified Gap / Opportunity | Recommended Solution |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | SEO & Search Snippet | `<meta name="description">` missing | Search engines display random sentence fragments instead of a structured overview. | Add an engaging meta description (e.g. "Meet the dedicated staff and Board of Directors driving Indigenous Tourism Manitoba, empowering Indigenous entrepreneurs across the province."). |
| 2 | Social Share Previews | Open Graph / Twitter cards missing | Shared links on LinkedIn, Facebook, or messaging platforms lack branded image cards. | Implement `og:title`, `og:description`, and `og:image` featuring a group or branded leadership graphic. |
| 3 | Community Representation | Profile cards display title only | Community affiliations / Treaty territories are only visible on individual child bio pages. | Add a subtitle tag or nation affiliation directly on the directory card (e.g. "Peguis First Nation", "Métis"). |
| 4 | Bottom Exit Funnel | Page ends after Board grid | Visitors reaching the bottom of the page have no closing CTA before the footer. | Repeat the "Connect With Our Team" CTA banner at the bottom of the board grid. |

---

## 3. Checklist Verification

- [x] **Page Purpose Clarity:** Immediate clarity on organizational governance and leadership.
- [ ] **Search Engine Metadata:** Meta description is missing.
- [ ] **Social Media Meta Tags:** Open Graph tags are missing.
- [x] **Primary Conversion Actions:** "Contact Us" and "Become a Member" buttons are present in the introductory section.
- [x] **Authority & Trust:** High credibility established through transparent leadership profiles.
