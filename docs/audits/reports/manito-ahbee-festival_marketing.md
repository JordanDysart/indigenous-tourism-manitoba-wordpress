# Audit Report: Manito Ahbee Festival — Marketing & Conversion

**Page URL:** `https://indigenoustourismmanitoba.ca/operator/manito-ahbee-festival/`  
**Audit Concern:** `marketing`  
**Date Audited:** `2026-08-30`  
**Conversion Readiness:** `Good (High Cultural Prestige; Event Ticketing & Schedule CTAs Recommended)`  

---

## 1. Executive Summary
The Manito Ahbee Festival profile highlights North America's premier celebration of Indigenous culture, pow wow dance, and artistic marketplace. The festival represents a marquee cultural tourism draw for Manitoba. Conversion can be substantially increased by introducing prominent "View Event Schedule" and "Get Festival Tickets" action buttons, displaying key annual dates, and deploying targeted search and Open Graph metadata.

---

## 2. Detailed Findings

| # | Focus Area | Current State | Identified Gap / Opportunity | Recommended Solution |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Event Conversion Funnel | Single generic "Visit Website" button | Attendees and cultural tourists look for direct access to schedules, ticketing, and competitor registration. | Implement prominent dual CTAs: *"View Festival Schedule"* & *"Get Festival Tickets"*. |
| 2 | Annual Date Callout | Dates omitted in overview header | Festival tourists planning travel itineraries need immediate clarity on festival dates (May long weekend). | Add a prominent "Upcoming Dates & Venue" ribbon in the sidebar. |
| 3 | Key Pillars Highlight | Described in running text | Visual badges for core festival pillars (International Pow Wow, Marketplace, Music Showcase, Spirit Run) improve scanning. | Add a "Festival Highlights at a Glance" badge section. |
| 4 | SEO & Search Snippet | `<meta name="description">` missing | Search engines lack a rich description of Manitoba's flagship Indigenous cultural festival. | Add a targeted meta description (e.g. "Experience the Manito Ahbee Festival in Winnipeg, Manitoba. Celebrate Indigenous culture, arts, music, and one of North America's largest international pow wows."). |
| 5 | Social Share Previews | Open Graph / Twitter cards missing | Shared links render generic social preview cards without imagery. | Implement standard `og:title`, `og:description`, and `og:image`. |
