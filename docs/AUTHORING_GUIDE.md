# ITM Staff Content Guide: Publishing News Mentions & Newsletters

This guide explains how any team member can quickly publish **News Mentions** and **Newsletters** on the Indigenous Tourism Manitoba website without needing technical or coding knowledge.

---

## Quick Reference Summary

| Content Type | Where It Appears | Category to Select | Key Feature |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **News Mention** | `/updates/`, `/category/news/`, and single post page | **News** | Locked outbound button linking to the publication article (e.g. CBC, Free Press). Supports backdating. |
| **Newsletter** | `/updates/`, `/category/newsletter/`, single post page, and the **Homepage Newsletter Highlight section** | **Newsletter** | Full newsletter edition layout with community highlights and direct subscription callouts. |

---

## 1. How to Post a News Mention (Press Coverage)

Use this whenever Indigenous Tourism Manitoba or our member operators are featured in newspapers, television, radio, online magazines, or external press.

### Step 1: Create the Post
1. Log in to your WordPress Admin dashboard.
2. In the left sidebar, click **Posts → Add New Post**.
3. A modal titled **"Choose a pattern"** will appear automatically.
4. Select the **News Mention** pattern.

### Step 2: Add Your Content
1. **Title**: Enter a clear headline at the top (e.g., `CBC News: Indigenous Tourism in Manitoba Experiencing Surge`).
2. **Heading**: Change `Featured in [Publication Name]` to the name of the publication (e.g., `Featured in CBC News` or `Winnipeg Free Press`).
3. **Summary / Write-Up**: Replace the placeholder text with your 2–3 paragraph write-up explaining what the story covered, highlighting any quoted operators or key takeaways for visitors.
4. **Outbound Story Button**:
   - Click the button that says **"Read the full story at [Publication]"**.
   - Change the button label (e.g., `Read the full story at CBC News`).
   - Click the **Link** icon in the toolbar and paste the direct URL to the external article.
   - *Note: This button is locked so it cannot be accidentally deleted, and is pre-configured to securely open in a new tab.*

### Step 3: Configure Sidebar Settings
On the right-hand sidebar under the **Post** tab:
1. **Categories**:
   - Check **News**.
   - Uncheck "Uncategorized" if checked.
2. **Publish Date (Backdating)**:
   - If the news coverage happened last month or last year, click on the **Publish** date (by default, `Immediately`).
   - Select the actual calendar date the article was published. The website will automatically sort the article into its correct chronological position!
3. **Excerpt**:
   - Expand the **Excerpt** panel in the sidebar.
   - Write a 1–2 sentence summary (e.g., `CBC News highlights Indigenous Tourism Manitoba and member operators in their feature on cultural tourism growth.`). This appears on the card preview on the `/updates/` page.
4. **Featured Image (Optional)**:
   - If you have an approved photo or publication logo screenshot, click **Set featured image** to attach it.

### Step 4: Publish
- Click the blue **Publish** button in the top-right corner.
- Confirm by clicking **Publish** again.

## 2. How to Post an e-Newsletter

Use this whenever a new seasonal or monthly edition of our community e-newsletter is released.

### Step 1: Create the Post
1. In the left sidebar, click **Posts → Add New Post**.
2. In the modal, select the **Newsletter** pattern.

### Step 2: Add Your Content
1. **Title**: Enter the issue name (e.g., `Fall 2026 Community Newsletter: Seasonal Gatherings & Updates`).
2. **Introductory Note**: Update the opening greeting and lead paragraph.
3. **Sections**:
   - Fill in the headings and paragraphs for **Community Highlights**, **Seasonal Experiences**, and **Member Spotlight**.
   - You can add images, quote blocks, or extra paragraphs as needed.
4. **Callout Box**: The pre-built callout box at the bottom already includes buttons inviting readers to subscribe to future editions or browse past archives.

### Step 3: Configure Sidebar Settings
On the right-hand sidebar under the **Post** tab:
1. **Categories**:
   - Check **Newsletter**.
2. **Publish Date**:
   - Keep the current date, or select the date the email newsletter was sent out.
3. **Excerpt**:
   - Expand the **Excerpt** panel.
   - Write a 1–2 sentence teaser (e.g., `Explore seasonal updates, operator milestones, and upcoming cultural gatherings in our Fall 2026 newsletter.`).
4. **Featured Image (Optional)**:
   - Set a high-quality landscape photo to accompany the issue.

### Step 4: Publish
- Click **Publish**.
- **Instant Homepage Update**: The moment you publish a newsletter, the **Newsletter Highlight section on the homepage automatically updates** to showcase this new edition with its date and title!

---

## 3. Where Everything Appears on the Website

1. **Updates ("View All") Page (`/updates/`)**:
   - Displays all News and Newsletters sorted from newest to oldest.
   - Visitors can click the filter pills at the top (**All**, **News**, **Newsletter**) to view only the content they want without leaving the page.
2. **News Archive (`/category/news/`)**:
   - Displays exclusively news write-ups and press mentions.
3. **Newsletter Archive (`/category/newsletter/`)**:
   - Displays all newsletter issues in chronological order.
4. **Homepage Newsletter Highlight**:
   - Automatically queries and features the latest published newsletter, with a direct button to view all newsletters.

---

## 4. Pre-Publish Checklist for Staff

Before hitting Publish on any post, take 10 seconds to verify:
- [ ] Did I choose the right pattern (**News Mention** or **Newsletter**)?
- [ ] Is the correct category checked (**News** or **Newsletter**)?
- [ ] If this is an older press mention, did I set the **Publish Date** to the original date?
- [ ] If this is a News Mention, does the external button link to the real article URL?
- [ ] Did I write a 1–2 sentence **Excerpt** for the card preview?
